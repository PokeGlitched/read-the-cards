READ THE CARDS

The cards hold your fate. The cards know you. But do you know the cards? Can you hold your own fate?
Find out in this tarot-inspired card-matching game where you have to race against the clock and your mistakes to successfully match up every card to its duplicate!
Try to match up all the pixel-art cards up with their pair before making 15 mistakes or spending 100 seconds.
But be careful! Every mistake costs you 2 seconds, but finding a matching pair gains you 3!

I created this game for Week 3 of Terra, with a theme of "spooky"! I thought of this idea based off of ouija board videos and ideas of controlling your own destiny, which I tried to make into a simple board game!

HOW TO PLAY
You can play this game in your browser at https://pokeglitched.github.io/read-the-cards/ .
The game will immeidiatly throw you into a round, so be ready! 
Clicking on a card will flip it over, at which point you can click another card to see if they match or not! 
Matching cards will be identical to each other, no tricks involved,
Try to match all 15 fifteen paris before running out of attempts or time!

FEATURES

- Original pixel-art card faces based off of tarot cards

- Countdown timer for a race against the clock
